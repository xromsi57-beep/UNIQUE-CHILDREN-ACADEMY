Leadership Message

"Building Strong Moral Character & Academic Excellence in Gothra"

Dear Parents and Students, Welcome to Unique Children Academy, Gothra. Our aim is to cultivate a learning environment where children are empowered with knowledge, discipline, and strong moral values. We understand the changing educational requirements of rural and semi-urban Rajasthan, which is why we provide robust instruction in both Hindi & English mediums from Class 1 to Class 8.

With dedicated teachers, sports opportunities, and smart digital study tools, we ensure every child in Gothra and nearby villages of Khetri receives top-tier foundation education.

School Overview

About Unique Children Academy

Unique Children Academy is a recognized private unaided elementary school located in Gothra, within the Khetri block of Jhunjhunu district, Rajasthan (Pincode: 333504).

With UDISE+ Code 08050600138, the institution provides holistic education from Class 1 to Class 8. We cater to students seeking instruction in either Hindi Medium or English Medium, ensuring conceptual clarity in mathematics, science, language, and social studies.
